02 System capabilities

Everything the guidance
office needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built with a modular, role-based architecture for schools of any size. Each module works independently yet integrates seamlessly into a single dashboard.

01

Student Records

Centralized directory with personal, academic, and guardian information — searchable, filterable, and fully audited.

  • Personal & academic info
  • Guardian contacts
  • Document uploads
02

Counseling Tracking

Document each session, monitor cases, and schedule follow-ups with timeline-based progress tracking.

  • Session notes & recommendations
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Counselor notes (private/public)
03

Referral Monitoring

From initial referral to closure — track priority, status changes, and counselor notes in one place.

  • Priority levels (low → urgent)
  • Timeline-based updates
  • Auto-notifications to counselors
04

Student Record Inventory

A single overview of every student record — counts, guidance coverage, cases, and referrals with visual breakdowns.

  • Record & coverage counts
  • Year level & course breakdowns
  • Case and referral totals
05

Reporting & Analytics

Generate and export CSV reports across student records, counseling, referrals, and user activity logs.

  • 4 report types
  • Date-range filtering
  • CSV / Excel exports
06

Role-Based Security

Fine-grained permissions for Administrators, Guidance Staff, and Counselors with full audit logging.

  • 3 user roles
  • Login lockout protection
  • Full system audit trail
